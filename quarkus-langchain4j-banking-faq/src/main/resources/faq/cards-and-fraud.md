# Cards and fraud

## Lost or stolen cards

If your debit or credit card is lost or stolen, lock it right away from the
Larkspur app (Cards, then Lock Card) or call the 24-hour card line at
1-800-555-0142. A replacement card arrives in 5 to 7 business days; expedited
delivery is $15 and arrives in 2 business days.

## Disputing a transaction

You can dispute a debit card transaction within 60 days of the statement date
it first appeared on. Larkspur issues provisional credit within 10 business
days while the investigation runs, and finishes most investigations within 45
days. Credit card disputes must be filed within 60 days as well.

## Fraud alerts

We text you when we see a suspicious card transaction. Reply NO to decline it
and freeze the card. Larkspur will never ask for your full card number, PIN, or
online banking password by phone, text, or email.

## Travel

You do not need to tell us before you travel inside the United States. For
international travel, add a travel notice in the app at least 2 days before you
leave so your card is not declined abroad. Foreign transaction fees are 1%.
