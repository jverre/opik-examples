# Accounts and fees

Larkspur Credit Union members can open an Everyday Checking account with a
minimum opening deposit of $25. There is no monthly fee as long as you receive
at least one direct deposit of $250 or more per statement cycle; otherwise the
monthly fee is $6.

## Overdrafts

Overdraft Courtesy covers debit card and ACH transactions up to $300 for a flat
fee of $22 per item. We charge at most three overdraft fees per day. Members
can link a Larkspur Savings account to cover overdrafts for a $5 transfer fee
instead. Fees are waived if the account is brought back above zero by the end
of the next business day.

## ATMs

Larkspur members can use any ATM in the SharedBranch network for free. Out of
network ATM withdrawals cost $2.50, and Larkspur refunds up to four of those
fees per month for members with Premier Checking.
