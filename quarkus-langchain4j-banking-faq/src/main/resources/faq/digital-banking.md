# Digital banking

## Enrolling

Download the Larkspur app or visit larkspur.example/online and choose Enroll.
You need your member number, the last four digits of your Social Security
number, and a mobile phone to receive a one-time passcode.

## Resetting your password

Choose Forgot Password on the sign-in screen and we will text a six-digit code
that is valid for 10 minutes. After 5 failed sign-in attempts your online
access is locked for 30 minutes. If you are still locked out, call member
services at 1-800-555-0177 (weekdays 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Eastern).

## Mobile check deposit

Deposit a check in the app by photographing the front and back. The daily
limit is $5,000 and the monthly limit is $20,000. Checks deposited before 5
p.m. Eastern on a business day are usually available the next business day.

## Zelle and transfers

Zelle payments are limited to $1,500 per day. Transfers between your own
Larkspur accounts are instant. External ACH transfers take 1 to 3 business
days, and wire transfers cost $20 outgoing domestic.
