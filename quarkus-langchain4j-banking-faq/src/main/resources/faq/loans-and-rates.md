# Loans and rates

## Auto loans

Larkspur auto loans start at 5.49% APR for terms up to 60 months with a credit
score of 720 or higher. Terms of 61 to 84 months start at 6.29% APR. We finance
up to 100% of the vehicle's value for new vehicles and up to 90% for used
vehicles that are 7 model years old or newer. Members who set up automatic
payments from a Larkspur checking account get a 0.25% rate discount.

## Personal loans

Personal loans range from $1,000 to $35,000 with fixed rates from 9.99% APR.
There is no origination fee and no prepayment penalty. Funds are usually
deposited the next business day after approval.

## Mortgages

Larkspur does not originate mortgages directly. We refer members to our
partner, Harborline Home Lending, which offers fixed and adjustable rate
mortgages. Ask a branch representative for an introduction.

## Applying

You can apply online or in any branch. Applications take about 10 minutes and
a hard credit inquiry is run only after you accept an offer.
