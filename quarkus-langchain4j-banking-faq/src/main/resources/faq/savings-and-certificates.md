# Savings and certificates

## Regular savings

Every member has a Regular Savings share account that requires a $5 minimum
balance, which also establishes your membership. Regular Savings earns 0.40%
APY. Larkspur does not limit the number of withdrawals from savings accounts.

## High-Yield Savings

High-Yield Savings earns 4.10% APY on balances of $2,500 or more and 0.75% APY
on balances below that. Interest compounds daily and is paid monthly.

## Certificates

| Term      | APY   | Minimum |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| 6 months  | 4.35% | $1,000  |
| 12 months | 4.60% | $1,000  |
| 24 months | 4.25% | $1,000  |

Withdrawing from a certificate before maturity costs 90 days of interest for
terms up to 12 months and 180 days of interest for longer terms. Certificates
renew automatically at maturity unless you tell us otherwise within the
10-day grace period.

## Insurance on deposits

Deposits at Larkspur are insured to at least $250,000 per member by the NCUA.
