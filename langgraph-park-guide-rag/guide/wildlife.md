# Wildlife and safety in Alder Hollow

## Bears

Black bears live throughout the park. Keep 100 yards from bears and 25 yards
from all other wildlife. Never leave food in a parked car or tent. Carrying
bear spray on the trails is recommended, and rangers sell canisters at the
Cedar Gate Visitor Center for $42.

## Drones and fishing

Flying drones is prohibited everywhere in the park. Fishing is allowed in
Opal Lake and the Hollow River with a state fishing license; only barbless
hooks may be used and all trout under 12 inches must be released.

## Emergencies

Call 911 from anywhere with cell service. Without service, go to the nearest
visitor center or ranger station. Rangers patrol Ridgeline Drive every day in
summer and every weekend in winter.
