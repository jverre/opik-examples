# Camping in Alder Hollow

## Campgrounds

Birchwater Campground has 64 sites with flush toilets and costs $28 per night.
Tamarack Flats has 18 tent-only sites with vault toilets and costs $18 per
night. Birchwater is open May 15 to October 15. Tamarack Flats is open
June 15 to September 15.

## Reservations

Reservations open six months ahead and are made through the park reservation
desk by phone. A reservation can be changed or cancelled up to 48 hours before
arrival for a full refund. Half of the sites at each campground are held for
first-come, first-served arrivals.

## Backcountry camping

A free backcountry permit is required for any overnight trip beyond the
campgrounds and is issued at the Cedar Gate Visitor Center up to one day ahead.
Groups are limited to six people. Campfires are not allowed in the backcountry.
Store all food in the bear canisters that rangers lend out with each permit.

## Campfires

Fires are allowed only in the metal rings at Birchwater and Tamarack Flats.
Firewood must be bought at the campground store; bringing wood from outside the
park is prohibited to prevent spreading pests.
