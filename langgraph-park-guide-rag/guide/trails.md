# Trails of Alder Hollow

Trails are rated easy, moderate or strenuous. All distances are round trip.

## Day hikes

- **Fernbank Loop** - 2.1 miles, easy, flat boardwalk, suitable for strollers.
- **Moss Falls** - 4.6 miles, moderate, 600 feet of climbing, best in spring
  when the falls run highest.
- **Granite Crown** - 9.8 miles, strenuous, 3,100 feet of climbing, above
  treeline for the last two miles. Start before 9 am to avoid afternoon storms.

## Pets and bikes

Dogs are allowed on the Fernbank Loop and on paved roads, on a leash no longer
than 6 feet. Dogs are not allowed on Moss Falls or Granite Crown. Bicycles are
allowed on paved roads and on Cedar Gate Road, but never on hiking trails.

## Trail safety

Carry at least two liters of water per person on any moderate or strenuous
trail. There is no drinking water on the trails themselves. Cell service is
only available near the Cedar Gate Visitor Center.
