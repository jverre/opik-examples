# Visiting Alder Hollow National Park

Alder Hollow is a fictional national park of cedar forest, glacial lakes and
granite ridges. The park is open year round, but most roads and trails are only
fully clear from June through September.

## Entrance fees and passes

A private vehicle pays $30 for a 7-day pass. Visitors on foot or bicycle pay $15
per person. The Hollow Annual Pass costs $75 and covers one vehicle for twelve
months. Children under 16 enter free. Fees are collected at the Cedar Gate and
the Lakeshore Gate; the Ridge Gate is unstaffed and uses a self-pay kiosk.

## Seasons and road closures

Ridgeline Drive closes to vehicles from November 1 to May 15 because of snow.
Cedar Gate Road stays open all winter but may require tire chains. Check the
conditions board at the Cedar Gate visitor center before driving in winter.

## Visitor centers and hours

The Cedar Gate Visitor Center is open 8 am to 6 pm in summer and 9 am to 4 pm
in winter. The Lakeshore Ranger Station is open only from June to September,
9 am to 5 pm. Both have restrooms, maps and drinking water.
