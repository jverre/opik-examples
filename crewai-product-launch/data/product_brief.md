# Launch file: Fernwick Glide (fictional)

All companies, products, and numbers below are fictional. They exist so the
crew has something concrete to plan against without touching the web.

## The product

- Product: Fernwick Glide, a folding electric scooter by Fernwick Mobility.
- Weight: 11.8 kg, folds to 82 cm in under 3 seconds; fits under a train seat.
- Range: 28 km per charge; removable battery charges in 3.5 hours.
- Planned price: $649. A $59 theft-lock bundle is optional.
- Launch window: March, in the UK and Germany only.
- Marketing budget: $180,000 for the first 90 days.
- Team: 2 marketers, 1 community manager, 1 part-time designer.
- Known weakness: no suspension, so rough cobblestones are uncomfortable.

## The buyer

- Core buyer: urban commuters aged 25-45 with a 3-8 km last-mile gap between
  station and office. 64% of them use rail or metro for the main leg.
- Top purchase drivers (900-person survey): beats a crowded bus (46%), carry it
  onto the train (33%), lower cost than a car or bike share (21%).
- Top objection: "I'll get it stolen" (41%), followed by "it's too heavy to
  carry" (27%).
- Commuters research for 3 weeks on average, mostly on YouTube and Reddit.

## Competitors

### StrideX S2 (market leader)

- Price: $599. Weight 14.5 kg. Range 25 km.
- Strength: 600 retail stores across Europe, so buyers can test-ride it.
- Weakness: heavy, and the fold takes about 12 seconds with a latch that
  trapped fingers in 7% of reviews.

### Voltaire Mini

- Price: $499. Weight 10.2 kg. Range 15 km.
- Strength: cheapest light scooter, very popular with students.
- Weakness: 15 km range fails on a round trip, and the battery is not
  removable, so it must be carried to a socket.

### Cobalt Roam

- Price: $899. Weight 13 kg. Range 40 km. Full suspension.
- Strength: the most comfortable ride on rough roads.
- Weakness: price, and it does not fit under a train seat when folded.

## Channels and what we know about them

- YouTube reviewer partnerships: about $6,000 per video, 8-12 videos is typical
  for a launch; reviewers drive the most research-stage traffic.
- Reddit and commuter forums: organic only, community manager time.
- Rail-station pop-up demos: $4,500 per day per site; 10 sites is a typical
  launch.
- Paid search and social ads: $18 average cost per click; 2.4% of clicks buy.
- Retail partnerships: none yet; a Berlin bike-shop chain has shown interest.
