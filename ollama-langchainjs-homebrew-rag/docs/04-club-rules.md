# Foxglove Hollow Brew Club: Brewing Day Rules

Club handbook sheet FH-04. Applies to every member using the shared kettle.

## Sanitation

Everything that touches wort after the boil must be sanitised with the club
no-rinse sanitiser at 5 ml per litre, with 2 minutes of contact time. Wash
first, sanitise second. Sanitiser does nothing on top of visible grime.

## Booking the kettle

The kettle and fermentation fridge are booked through the sign-up board,
for blocks of 6 hours. A brewing day must finish by 20:00 because the
hall closes at 21:00 and cleaning takes an hour. Members who cancel less than
48 hours ahead lose one booking credit.

## Labelling and tasting nights

Every fermenter and every crate of bottles gets a label with the style, the
brew date and your initials. The club holds a tasting night on the last Friday
of each month, and every member must bring two bottles. Unlabelled bottles are
poured out.
