# Foxglove Hollow Brew Club: Bottling and Conditioning

Club handbook sheet FH-03. Read this before your first bottling day.

## Priming sugar

Prime each 500 ml bottle with 3 g of table sugar, or use 120 g of sugar
dissolved in 250 ml of boiling water for the whole 20-litre batch. Stir the
syrup in gently with a sanitised spoon. Stirring hard introduces oxygen and
stales the beer within weeks.

## Conditioning time

Store bottles at 20 C for 2 weeks to carbonate, then move them to the cool
cellar for at least 1 more week. Test one bottle at the 2 week mark. If it is
flat, give the batch another week before opening a second one.

## Bottle safety

Use only club-approved amber bottles with a pressure rating of 8 bar or more.
Never reuse bottles that held sparkling cider or lemonade. Store carbonating
bottles in a lidded crate, because an over-primed bottle can burst, and
shards fly.
