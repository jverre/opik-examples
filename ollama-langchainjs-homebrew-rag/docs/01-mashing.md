# Foxglove Hollow Brew Club: Mashing Guide

Club handbook sheet FH-01. Written for the 20-litre club kettle.

## Mash temperature

Mash the Hollow Amber and the Moss Porter at 67 C for 60 minutes. A lower
mash at 64 C gives a drier, more fermentable wort and is used only for the
Pale Lantern. Check the temperature after 10 minutes and stir in a splash of
hot water if it has dropped more than 2 degrees.

## Water volumes

Use 3 litres of strike water per kilogram of grain. Heat the strike water to
72 C, which settles at about 67 C once the grain goes in. Sparge with 76 C
water, never hotter, because hot sparge water pulls tannins out of the husks.

## Grain milling

The club mill is set to a 1.1 mm gap. Crush the grain the day you brew, not
earlier, and keep the husks intact. A flour-fine crush causes a stuck
sparge, and the only fix is to scoop out the mash and start the runoff again.
