# Foxglove Hollow Brew Club: Fermentation Guide

Club handbook sheet FH-02. Applies to all 20-litre club batches.

## Pitching yeast

Rehydrate one 11 g sachet of dry ale yeast in 100 ml of 30 C boiled water
for 15 minutes, then pitch into wort cooled to 19 C. Pitching into wort
warmer than 22 C is forbidden because it throws off fruity esters the club
styles do not want.

## Fermentation temperature

Hold the fermenter at 18 C for the first 3 days, then let it rise to 20 C to
finish. The club fermentation fridge lives in the cellar and is booked on the
sign-up board. Never move a fermenter into a warm room to speed things up.

## Gravity readings

Take the original gravity before pitching and the final gravity after 14
days. The Hollow Amber should start near 1.052 and finish near 1.012. Two
identical readings three days apart mean fermentation is done. Do not bottle
on a single reading.
