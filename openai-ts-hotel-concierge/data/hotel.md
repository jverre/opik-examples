# The Driftwood Pier Hotel, Tern Harbor (fictional)

## Rooms
- Harbor View Double: 189 EUR per night, breakfast not included.
- Lighthouse Suite: 340 EUR per night, breakfast included, balcony facing the pier.
- Dune Cottage (sleeps 4): 275 EUR per night, kitchenette, dogs allowed (25 EUR flat fee per stay).

## Times and policies
- Check-in from 15:00, check-out by 11:00. Late check-out until 14:00 costs 40 EUR.
- Free cancellation up to 48 hours before arrival.
- Breakfast: 07:00 to 10:30 in the Gull Room, 18 EUR per person.
- Parking: 12 EUR per night in the Net Loft garage, 6 spaces only.

## Amenities
- The Salt Spa is open 10:00 to 20:00. A 50-minute seaweed wrap is 85 EUR.
- The heated saltwater pool is open 08:00 to 21:00 and is free for guests.
- The ferry to Cormorant Island leaves the hotel pier at 09:30 and 14:00 daily, 14 EUR return.

## Restaurant
- Pilot House restaurant serves dinner 18:00 to 22:00, closed Mondays.
- Tables are checked with the availability tool; do not guess availability.
