# Spoke & Sprocket Cycle Works (fictional)

A neighbourhood bike repair shop at 14 Quarry Lane, Pebble Ford.

## Hours
- Open Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00.
- Closed Sunday and Monday.
- Drop-offs need no appointment. Bring the bike in during opening hours.

## Services and prices (labour only, parts are extra)
- Flat tire fix (tube swap and tire check): $18
- Basic tune-up (brakes, gears, tire pressure, chain lube): $65
- Full overhaul (everything in the tune-up, plus drivetrain clean, wheel true, bearing service): $180
- Hydraulic brake bleed: $40 per pair
- Wheel true: $25 per wheel
- Chain replacement: $15 labour plus the chain (chains start at $28)
- E-bike diagnostics: $45. We can diagnose battery and motor faults but we do not repair motors or batteries; we refer those to the maker.

## Turnaround and policy
- Standard turnaround is 2 business days. Overhauls take 4 business days.
- We always send a quote by text before starting any work beyond the listed service.
- Bikes left more than 7 days after we tell you they are ready cost $15 per week to store.
- Repairs carry a 30-day warranty on our labour.
- We do not repair bikes with carbon frame cracks; we will tell you honestly if a frame is unsafe.
