# Survey Protocols and Priorities - Marrow Fen Reserve

How the reserve team runs surveys and decides where to spend a small budget.

## Reserve priorities

- The reserve ranks species by trend, then by how few places the species
  lives, then by whether the team can act on the threat in one season.
- Management budget for next season: enough for one new fenced exclosure, one
  signed buffer zone, or ten more camera units, but not all three.

## Known limitations

- Transect counts and camera detections are different measures and should not
  be added together or compared as raw numbers.
- A survey with fewer than five walks per transect is flagged as low
  confidence and is not used alone to declare a trend.

## Past actions

- In 2023 the team signed a 200 m buffer around the harrier nests. Harrier
  pairs rose from 4 to 6 since then.
- The drainage board must give 60 days' notice before any reed cutting, but
  last winter's notice was sent after cutting had started.
