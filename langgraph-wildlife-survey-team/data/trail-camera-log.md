# Trail Camera Log - Marrow Fen Reserve (survey season 2025)

Twelve motion cameras ran from April to October. Counts are independent
detections, where a species photographed again within 30 minutes counts once.

## Detections by species

- Fen otter: 87 detections on 6 cameras, mostly at dawn.
- Red fox: 203 detections on 11 of 12 cameras.
- Water vole: 9 detections, all on the two upstream cameras.
- Lantern bittern: 2 detections, both on camera C7 beside the bittern nest.

## Camera problems

- Cameras C3 and C9 were stolen in August, so their data end on 11 August.
- C5 flooded in June and lost three weeks of images.

## Human activity

- 1,460 detections of people or dogs; 38 percent of dog detections were
  off-lead, mostly on the east flank and the Heron Causeway footpath.
- Foxes were seen carrying eggs on 4 occasions near the fen edge.
