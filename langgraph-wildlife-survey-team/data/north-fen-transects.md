# North Fen Transects - Marrow Fen Reserve (survey season 2025)

Walked transects across the open sedge fen north of Heron Causeway. All counts
are birds detected per 10 km of transect, averaged over six walks.

## Summary of counts

- Ember-throated wren: 4.1 per 10 km, down from 7.8 in 2023.
- Sedge crake: 2.6 per 10 km, steady against 2024.
- Lantern bittern: 0.9 per 10 km; one confirmed nest, the first since 2021.
- Pale-bellied snipe: 11.4 per 10 km, the most common wader on the fen.

## Habitat notes

- Reed cutting by the drainage board removed about 12 hectares of tall sedge
  along Heron Causeway in February, right where the wrens nest.
- Water levels stayed high through June, which suited the crake and the snipe.
- Two transects (N3 and N4) were skipped in week five because of flooding, so
  their counts rest on five walks, not six.

## Observer comments

- Wren song was thin everywhere south of marker N2; the observers suspect the
  cutting pushed pairs north into thicker cover.
- The bittern nest sits 300 m from the causeway footpath and is not signed.
