# Cold Spring Creek Survey - Marrow Fen Reserve (survey season 2025)

Amphibian and small mammal work along Cold Spring Creek, using funnel traps
and night-time listening points.

## Amphibians

- Fen tree frog: calling males at 19 of 24 listening points, compared with
  22 of 24 in 2022.
- Spotted newt: 140 animals caught across 60 trap nights, a record year.
- Egg-mass counts for the tree frog fell 30 percent in the lower creek, where
  the pool dried out by early July.

## Small mammals

- Fen otter: fresh spraint at 5 of 8 creek crossings, up from 3 of 8 in 2024.
- Water vole: signs at only 2 crossings, both upstream of the old weir.
- No mink were caught or recorded this season.

## Water quality

- The lower creek ran at 19 degrees C in July, three degrees warmer than the
  upper creek, which is shaded by alder.
