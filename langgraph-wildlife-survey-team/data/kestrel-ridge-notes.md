# Kestrel Ridge Field Notes - Marrow Fen Reserve (survey season 2025)

Notes from the raptor watch on Kestrel Ridge, run by two volunteers from March
to September, four mornings a week.

## Raptor counts

- Banded kestrel: 31 breeding pairs, up from 24 in 2024.
- Marsh harrier: 6 pairs, with 11 fledglings counted in August.
- Dusk owl: heard on only 3 of 40 evening visits, the lowest rate on record.

## Threats and disturbance

- A new mountain-bike route opened in May along the east flank. Volunteers
  logged 14 cases of riders passing within 50 m of banded kestrel nest boxes.
- Two harrier nests failed in June; observers suspect off-lead dogs but
  recorded no direct evidence.

## Coverage caveats

- The watch lost a volunteer in July, so the second half of the season has
  fewer observer hours than the first.
- Dusk owl visits are weather dependent; 9 evenings were abandoned for rain.
